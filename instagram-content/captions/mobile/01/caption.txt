Same business.
Two completely different lives.

Left side is most small business owners I know —
busy, reactive, everything living in their head.

Right side is what happens when you stop relying on memory
and start relying on a system.

Leads get followed up.
Work gets delivered the same way every time.
You can finally step back without everything falling apart.

You don't need to work harder.
You need a system that works when you're tired.

If you're stuck on the left — comment "SYSTEM".
