"I'm busy but broke."

Not because they don't work hard.
Because the business only moves when they move.

No follow-up path.

No handoff process.

So revenue stays random.
And stress stays permanent.

Install a system and the shift looks boring on paper:

But that boring loop is how you stop being the bottleneck…
and start earning like the business can breathe without you.
