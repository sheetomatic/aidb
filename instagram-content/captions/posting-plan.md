## Day 5 — Reel script (talking head)

**Hook (0–3s):**
"You're not bad at business. You just don't have a system."

**Problem (3–12s):**
"Most small business owners I know are exhausted… not because they're lazy, but because every lead, every delivery, every decision still runs through them."

**Turn (12–20s):**
"A system is just a written path: lead comes in, follow-up happens, delivery has a checklist, review gets asked. Same steps. Every time."

**Payoff (20–28s):**
"When the work stops living in your head, you stop being the bottleneck. That's when you can actually scale — and earn more without working more hours."

**CTA:**
"Comment SYSTEM if you want the starter checklist."
